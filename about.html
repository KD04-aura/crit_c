Our Story

Welcome to Persian Treasures. Our business focuses on Persian decorations, traditional handicrafts and home accessories.

We aim to share the beauty of Persian culture through traditional patterns, detailed artwork and unique products.

Our Products

Our collection includes Persian rugs, decorative bath mats, miniature paintings, Khatamkari boxes and Iranian football kits.

Our Aim

Our aim is to make Persian products easier to discover and help customers learn more about their designs and craftsmanship.
